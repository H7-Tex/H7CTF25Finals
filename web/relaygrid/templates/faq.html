Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about RelayGrid Email (and then some)

💡 What is RelayGrid?

RelayGrid is a modern email client that delivers your messages faster than your internet provider on a good day. We handle the technical stuff so you can focus on what matters: your communication. No bloated features, just fast, reliable email service.

💰 Is RelayGrid free?

Absolutely! Unless you want to pay us, in which case, we won't stop you. Free email storage, unlimited sending, no hidden costs, no surprise subscriptions. Just fast, reliable email service that doesn't cost a dime.

📝 How do I sign up?

Click the "Signup" link in the navigation bar above. Fill out the simple form with your desired email address and password. That's it! You'll be sending emails within seconds. No lengthy verification processes or jumping through hoops.

🌙 Is there a dark mode?

Yes! RelayGrid is permanently in dark mode. Your eyes will thank you after those late-night email sessions. We believe in protecting your retinas while you manage your inbox, because squinting at bright screens is so last decade.

⚡ How fast is RelayGrid?

Faster than your morning coffee kicks in. Email delivery happens in milliseconds, not minutes. Your inbox loads instantly, messages send before you can second-guess them, and searching through thousands of emails feels effortless.

🔒 Is my data secure?

Your emails are safer here than your embarrassing childhood photos in your parents' attic. We use end-to-end encryption, secure servers, and regular security audits. Your private conversations stay private, no snooping allowed.
